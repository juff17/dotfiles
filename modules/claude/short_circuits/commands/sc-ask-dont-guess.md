---
description: Ask me the open questions instead of assuming an answer.
---

When something is unclear, ambiguous, or blocking, stop and ask — don't guess, invent a fact, or paper over the gap with a silent assumption. Surface the open question directly (use AskQuestion where it fits) and wait for my answer. Never write an assumption down as though it were established truth; if you must move forward on one, flag it explicitly as an assumption to confirm rather than burying it in the work.
