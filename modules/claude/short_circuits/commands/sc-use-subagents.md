---
description: Farm independent and research work out to subagents.
---

Reach for subagents to keep the main context clean and move faster. Spawn them for independent or parallelizable work, for research and investigation, and to pressure-test or challenge an approach before we commit to it — each one returning just its decision, finding, or result. Prefer this over doing serial investigation inline yourself, especially when a question is self-contained or when you're about to read widely to answer it. Give any subagent the same standing constraints you're operating under.
